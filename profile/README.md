# DCV Applied Technology

DCV Applied Technology, LLC is an Ohio company doing research, development
and commercialization of novel firearms-related technology: industrial-grade
engineering applied to the firearms space. The company, its products and
contact details are at **[dcv-at.com](https://dcv-at.com)**.

This organisation holds the parts of that work that are open: printed shop
hardware released as open hardware, and the bench tooling built along the way.

## Open hardware (CERN-OHL-S v2)

| Project | What it is |
|---|---|
| [para-buckle](https://github.com/dcvappliedtechnologyllc/para-buckle) | Printed paracord-to-webbing sling adapters in 1.00 in and 1.25 in. Parametric CadQuery sources, STL and STEP. |
| [doyle-vee-jaws](https://github.com/dcvappliedtechnologyllc/doyle-vee-jaws) | Printed light-vee soft jaws, keel dock and handwheel for the Harbor Freight Doyle 4-1/2 in vise. Parametric OpenSCAD and STL. |

## Tools (MIT)

| Project | What it is |
|---|---|
| [camlab](https://github.com/dcvappliedtechnologyllc/camlab) | Claude-in-the-loop camera and lighting optimizer for a tethered Canon and Godox mesh lights, with reusable camera and light control layers. |
| [trimlab](https://github.com/dcvappliedtechnologyllc/trimlab) | One-clip video trimmer for macOS: pywebview UI over a Python/ffmpeg core. |

Everything here is built for our own bench first and published as-is. Issues
and pull requests are welcome; the licence of each repository governs reuse.
